How to use this report

This service uses statistical and artificial intelligence methods based on age, lifestyle, family history, past medical history, and other health information self-reported by the user to provide personalized organization of cancer-related risk factors and health education information. The result does not represent the probability of developing cancer and is not intended for cancer diagnosis, screening, early detection, disease prediction, or treatment decision-making. It also cannot replace a physician's evaluation or any standard medical examination.
